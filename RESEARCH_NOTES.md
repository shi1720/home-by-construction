# Research scope and evidence notes

## Novelty search and nearest work

The search was conducted on 14 October 2026. It was a focused source review, not a PRISMA systematic review or an exhaustive patent search. Queries included street-art origin and artist mobility measurement; Street Art Cities nationality; WIPO origin/hunter reporting bias; volunteered geographic information contributor bias and leave-one-contributor-out analysis; cultural mapping and partial identification; majority-country artist-origin sensitivity; measurement fairness and circular analysis. Primary publications and platform/organization documentation were inspected. Full PDFs were inspected for the WIPO technical note, the cultural-mapping review, Measurement and Fairness, and Fairness and Abstraction in Sociotechnical Systems. Other literature use is confined to the claims supported by the inspected publication pages, abstracts, or accessible texts.

Absence from a finite search is not evidence of universal absence. ## Prior manuscript overlap screen

The local research folder's paper index and the extracted text of all top-level PDFs were inspected. The existing papers cover tool caches, AI delegation, memory, financial exposures, noisy bandits, retrieval duplication, access quotas, schema fidelity, medical evidence, control, historical timber accounts, infrastructure permissions, and expression attribution. None of the inspected manuscripts studies street-art data, artist origin, collector concentration, or cultural mobility indicators. The present paper uses generic audit and optimization techniques also used elsewhere; their use alone is not a distinct novelty claim. No earlier manuscript or experiment was repackaged, and no existing task or repository was modified. Screening is bounded by the available folder; unpublished work elsewhere could not be ruled out.

## Commercial and institutional context

The source platform already supplies City Insights and municipal partnership products, plus an artist directory used by partner cities. This establishes an actual category of customers and a plausible integration point for indicator audits.

Sources:
- https://streetartcities.com/about/partners
- https://streetartcities.substack.com/p/a-new-chapter-street-art-cities-launches
- https://streetart.community/t/state-of-the-business-january-2026/3095

The January 2026 business statement reports approximately EUR 12,000 of municipal partnership revenue and EUR 10,000 of supporter revenue for 2025. These source-reported figures suggest a small existing niche, not evidence for a large addressable market. No interviews, demand estimation, sales validation, or claim of patentability were performed. A commercially useful product would be a data-agnostic measurement-audit component coupled to properly licensed data and artist correction mechanisms. Dataset access rights would need to be negotiated for non-academic use.

## Research integrity

All primary results come from downloaded CSV exports and executable computations. The only synthetic numerical input is an explicitly labeled CLI example and the finite/exhaustive correctness tests. Completion additions are declared counterfactuals. Nationality tags are unverified comparators; missing-origin biographies remain unknown. A local analysis plan was created before outcomes but after schema inspection, with no external preregistration. Full author review remains necessary before making submission attestations about the work. The AI disclosure is brief but accurately covers its substantive role.
